  • Completely blocks pre-roll/mid-roll video ads, banners and sponsored ads — not just on YouTube, but on ANY movie/video site.
  • Auto-detects fake bank/social-media/e-wallet sites with a self-learning algorithm — catches brand-new impersonation domains never seen on any blocklist before, PLUS flags financial-scam pages by content (OTP/password form + scam language) even when they don't impersonate any known brand.
  • Instant warning when a known malware-distributing site is detected, plus automatic flag/block of risky downloaded files.
  • 3-mode Element Zapper: Left-click to instantly destroy any ad that slips through, Right-click to open a smart panel listing every layer at that spot with live preview so you pick exactly what to remove, Alt+Ctrl+Right-click to protect real content forever — every action is logged so you can export it to share with the developer.
  • Blocks popup/popunder windows and click-hijack links that wrap a real video or button to redirect you to gambling/affiliate sites — detected by structure, no need to know the target domain in advance.
  • Advanced fingerprint cloaking: fakes AudioContext/WebGL/Canvas/hardware info, AND disguises every faked function's `.toString()`/`.name`/`.length` to look exactly native — passes even sophisticated bank/Cloudflare-grade anti-fraud scanners.
  • Actively defeats video freezing and background bandwidth throttling — auto-forces playback to resume when stuck.
  • Instant Pause: turn OFF all ad blocking on the current tab immediately, no page reload — flip it back on just as instantly when you're done.
  • Exclude an entire site with one right-click when a page breaks before a proper fix ships — no waiting for an update, re-enable it anytime from the same menu.
